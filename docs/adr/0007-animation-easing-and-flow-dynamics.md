# Animation Easing and Flow Dynamics

World in Layers will support non-linear keyframe easing (`linear`, `ease-in`, `ease-out`, `ease-in-out`, `cubic-in`, `cubic-out`, `cubic-in-out`) and animatable `stroke_dashoffset` / `stroke_dasharray` flow dynamics directly in the declarative Scene Sequence schema. This brings tactile, organic motion deceleration and fluid directional flow to geographic systems (rivers, tidal pressure vectors, drainage paths, camera transitions) while maintaining strict offline determinism, backwards compatibility, and zero new runtime dependencies.
