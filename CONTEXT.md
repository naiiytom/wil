# World in Layers

World in Layers is a creator-operated framework for producing Paper Dioramas for a faceless YouTube channel of the same name. Its initial output supports episodes of roughly eight minutes about geographic systems and infrastructure.

## Language

**Creator**:
The person who assembles visual components into an episode. Initially, this is the project owner.
_Avoid_: end user, customer

**Episode**:
An approximately eight-minute video published on the World in Layers channel.
_Avoid_: project, montage

**Paper Diorama**:
A standalone layered-paper visual made for use in an Episode.
_Avoid_: paper indorama, visual component, building block

**Scene**:
A shot-ready Paper Diorama that communicates one visual idea in an Episode.
_Avoid_: montage, sequence

**Layer**:
A named visual part of a Scene that remains independently addressable for later animation.
_Avoid_: flattened asset

**Source-backed**:
Grounded in verifiable geographic or factual source material, while remaining visually simplified.
_Avoid_: photorealistic, literal

**Source Pack**:
The collection of commercially usable geographic material, citations, and editorial values that grounds a Scene.
_Avoid_: live data feed

**Source Reference**:
The source, license, and retrieval information for one dataset or factual claim group used by a Source Pack.
_Avoid_: uncited source, general bibliography

**AI Material**:
An AI-generated non-factual surface treatment used to give a Scene a paper-like physical quality.
_Avoid_: geographic evidence, data visualization

**Scene Render**:
A 16:9 4K image exported from a Scene for assembly into an Episode.
_Avoid_: montage, animation

**Label**:
Source-backed text embedded in a Scene to identify or explain its geographic system.
_Avoid_: narration, end card
